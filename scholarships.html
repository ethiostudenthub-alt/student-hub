Scholarships

Opportunities to help fund your studies, in Ethiopia and abroad.

DAAD Scholarships (Germany)

Germany's DAAD program funds master's and PhD study for students from many countries, including Ethiopia, and has dedicated programs for African applicants. Germany's public universities also charge no tuition for international students.

Visit DAAD

Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program

Funds university education for African students at partner universities across Africa and abroad, with a focus on academic promise and leadership potential.

Visit Mastercard Foundation

Chevening Scholarships (UK)

The UK government's fully funded master's scholarship for future leaders, open to students from many countries including Ethiopia, covering tuition, living costs, and flights.

Visit Chevening

Commonwealth Scholarships (UK)

Funded by the UK government for students from Commonwealth countries, covering master's and PhD study, with an aim toward students who otherwise couldn't afford to study in the UK.

Visit Commonwealth Scholarships

MEXT Scholarship (Japan)

The Japanese government's scholarship for international undergraduate and graduate students, covering tuition, a monthly stipend, and travel costs.

Visit JASSO

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

Fully funded scholarships to study in China at the undergraduate, master's, or PhD level, open to students from many countries including Ethiopia.

Visit CampusChina

Fulbright Foreign Student Program (USA)

Funds graduate-level study and research in the United States for international students, including a living stipend and travel costs.

Visit Fulbright

Australia Awards

Funded by the Australian government for students from partner countries including Ethiopia, covering full tuition, living costs, and travel for study in Australia.

Visit Australia Awards

Türkiye Bursları (Turkey)

The Turkish government's scholarship program for international students, covering tuition, accommodation, a monthly stipend, and Turkish language training.

Visit Türkiye Scholarships

Global Korea Scholarship

Funded by the South Korean government for undergraduate and graduate international students, covering tuition, airfare, and a monthly allowance.

Visit Study in Korea

Swedish Institute Scholarships

Funds master's study in Sweden for students from selected countries, with a focus on future leaders and change-makers.

Visit Swedish Institute

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EU)

Fully funded joint master's programs across multiple European universities, open to students worldwide, covering tuition, travel, and a monthly stipend.

Visit Erasmus Mundus Catalogue

African Leadership University (Rwanda/Mauritius)

Offers substantial need-based financial aid to African students for undergraduate study, alongside a focus on entrepreneurship and leadership.

Visit ALU

Rhodes Scholarships (University of Oxford)

Extremely competitive but fully funded postgraduate scholarship at Oxford, with dedicated regional selection processes for African applicants.

Visit Rhodes Trust

Need-Blind US Universities (Harvard, Yale, Princeton, MIT, and others)

A very short list of US universities — including Harvard, Yale, Princeton, MIT, Amherst, Dartmouth, Bowdoin, Notre Dame, Brown, and Washington and Lee — admit international students without considering ability to pay, then cover 100% of demonstrated financial need if admitted. Genuinely life-changing if it works out, but extremely competitive: acceptance rates are typically under 5-7%, even lower for international applicants.
