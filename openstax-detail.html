OpenStax

Free, peer-reviewed college textbooks.

What it is

OpenStax is a nonprofit publisher based at Rice University that produces free, peer-reviewed textbooks for college-level courses, written and reviewed by real professors.

Subjects available

Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Calculus, Statistics, Economics, Psychology, Business, and more — complete textbooks, not summaries.

How to use it

Read directly in your browser for free, or download a PDF version to keep and read offline. No account required to read online.

Good to know

Written for a US college curriculum, so some examples may differ slightly from Ethiopian coursework, but core subject material is the same worldwide.
