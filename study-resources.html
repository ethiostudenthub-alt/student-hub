Study Resources

Notes, guides, and materials to help with your coursework.

Khan Academy

Free video lessons and practice problems covering math, science, and more, useful for building or reviewing core subject knowledge.

Visit Khan Academy

OpenStax

Free, peer-reviewed textbooks covering subjects like biology, physics, chemistry, and math, available to read online or download as PDFs.

Visit OpenStax

MIT OpenCourseWare

Free lecture notes, exams, and course materials from actual MIT courses, across nearly every subject.

Visit MIT OpenCourseWare

Wikipedia

A useful starting point for background reading and understanding on almost any topic — always worth cross-checking with other sources.

Visit Wikipedia

YouTube (EDU channels)

Channels like CrashCourse, 3Blue1Brown, and Professor Leonard offer free, clearly explained lessons across science, math, and history.

Explore YouTube Education

Temari — Ethiopian University Entrance Exam Prep

Free timed practice papers for the Ethiopian University Entrance Examination (EUEE), covering both Natural and Social Science streams, with instant scoring.

Visit Temari

EthioExam

Free past exam papers, practice questions, and step-by-step solutions specifically for Ethiopian Grade 12 students.

Visit EthioExam

Ardiland — Amharic Exam Prep

Free past papers, study guides, and video tutorials in Amharic for Ethiopia's Grade 12 exams, useful if you prefer studying in Amharic.

Visit Ardiland

Exam Galaxy — Ethio Grade 12 Entrance Exam (App)

A mobile app with organized past exam questions and short curriculum notes for Ethiopian Grade 12 and university entrance exam prep.

Get it on Google Play

Ethio Digital Library (App) — verify before use

Several apps share this name from different developers, offering Ethiopian textbooks from Grade 1 to university freshman level. Because listings conflict, search "Ethio Digital Library" directly in your app store and check reviews/downloads before trusting one.

Search your app store

Project Gutenberg (Free Books)

Over 70,000 free books, mostly classic literature and older non-fiction whose copyright has expired, available to read online or download.

Visit Project Gutenberg
