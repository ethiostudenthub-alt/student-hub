DAAD Scholarships

Germany's national scholarship agency for international students.

What it is

DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service) is Germany's main organization for funding international students and researchers. It offers scholarships covering master's and PhD study, and in some cases short research stays.

What it covers

Most DAAD scholarships include a monthly stipend, health insurance, and travel costs. Germany's public universities also charge no tuition for international students regardless of scholarship status.

Who it's for

DAAD has specific programs aimed at students from developing countries, including dedicated tracks for African applicants.

How to apply

Applications are made directly through the DAAD website, usually 6-12 months before the intended start date.
