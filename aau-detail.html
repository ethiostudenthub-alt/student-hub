Addis Ababa University

Ethiopia's oldest and highest-ranked university.

Overview

Founded in 1950, Addis Ababa University (AAU) is Ethiopia's first modern university and remains its most prominent, with the widest range of programs in the country.

Programs offered

AAU offers programs across nearly every field — natural sciences, engineering, medicine, law, humanities, business, and social sciences.

Admission

Undergraduate admission is based on the Ethiopian University Entrance Examination (EUEE) score, with the Ministry of Education assigning students based on results.

Good to know

As Ethiopia's flagship university, AAU is generally the most competitive to gain admission to for high-demand programs like medicine and engineering.
