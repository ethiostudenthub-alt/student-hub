Temari

Free practice papers for the Ethiopian University Entrance Examination.

What it is

Temari offers timed, practice-exam-style papers modeled on the Ethiopian University Entrance Examination (EUEE), for realistic exam conditions rather than just reading notes.

Streams covered

Both the Natural Science stream and Social Science stream are covered.

How to use it

Take a timed practice paper under exam-like conditions, then get instant scoring so you know which areas need more review.
