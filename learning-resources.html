Free Learning & Career Resources

Free courses and resources to build skills for your career.

freeCodeCamp

Free, self-paced coding courses covering web development, Python, data analysis, and more, with certificates on completion.

Visit freeCodeCamp

Khan Academy

Free courses in math, science, computing, and test prep, taught through short videos and practice exercises.

Visit Khan Academy

Coursera (Audit / Financial Aid)

University-level courses from schools worldwide. Many can be audited for free, and financial aid is available for certificates.

Visit Coursera

edX

Free courses from universities like Harvard and MIT, including CS50, one of the most popular free computer science courses available online.

Visit edX

ALX Africa

Tech and career-readiness programs designed specifically for African learners, including software engineering and data science tracks.

Visit ALX Africa

Google Career Certificates

Career-focused certificate programs in IT support, data analytics, UX design, and more, with flexible free-audit options.

Visit Grow with Google

Google Digital Skills for Africa

Free courses designed specifically for African learners, focused on digital marketing, career development, and online business skills — not just coding.

Visit Grow with Google (Africa)

HP LIFE

Free business and entrepreneurship courses — covering topics like starting a business, marketing, and finance — aimed at anyone wanting practical business skills.

Visit HP LIFE

HubSpot Academy

Free certification courses in marketing, sales, and customer service — useful for anyone interested in business or digital marketing careers, not just tech.

Visit HubSpot Academy

Alison

Thousands of free short courses and diplomas across business, health, IT, and personal development, with free certificates on completion.

Visit Alison
