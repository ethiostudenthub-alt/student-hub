Duolingo

Free, gamified language learning.

What it is

Duolingo is a free app and website that teaches languages through short, game-like daily lessons.

What you can learn

English is one of its most popular courses, alongside dozens of other languages.

How to use it

Create a free account, pick English as your target, and complete short daily lessons — most take just 5-10 minutes.

Good to know

The free version includes ads and a limited number of mistakes per lesson, but is fully usable without paying.
